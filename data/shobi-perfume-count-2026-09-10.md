# Shobi official EN — wearable perfume count snapshot

**Snapshot date:** 2026-09-10
**Official source:** https://leparfum.com.gr/en/perfumes
**Language/catalog:** English (EN)

## Current working count

**2,337 Shobi perfume references**

This snapshot records the current working count obtained from the official English Shobi/LeParfum catalog, considering the personal/wearable perfume categories:

- Fragrances For Women: 855
- Fragrances For Men: 382
- Elegants Fragrances: 448
- Niche Perfumes: 612
- Luxury Perfumes: 40

Total: **2,337**

Excluded from this count:

- third-party perfume brands sold by the store
- home fragrances / room fragrances
- car fragrances / air fresheners
- candles / diffusers
- accessories / cosmetics
- Layered Perfumes (8), treated separately because they are combinations rather than standalone source perfume identities
- Musk (5), treated separately from the standard Shobi inspired-perfume identity catalog

## Important status

This is a **dated catalog-count snapshot / working baseline**, not yet a row-by-row deduplicated database of 2,337 product records. The individual Shobi codes still need to be enumerated and checked for cross-category duplication before calling 2,337 a fully certified unique-code count.
